# DU Spot Admission 2026: How to Get a Seat After Missing All Regular Rounds


## Quick Summary

- Spot Admission is DU's last-chance round for seats left vacant after all regular CSAS UG allocation rounds end.
- Only candidates who registered for CSAS UG 2026 and are not currently admitted anywhere can apply.
- There's no upgrade or withdrawal option in spot rounds — once you accept a seat, it's final.
- Seats are usually limited to specific colleges and programmes, so flexibility matters more than preference.
- You must act fast: spot rounds run on tight, same-day-to-few-day windows.

## Missed Every DU Allocation Round? You're Not Out Yet

If you're refreshing the CSAS portal wondering whether your DU dream is over because you didn't get a seat in the regular rounds, take a breath. Every year, thousands of seats across DU's 65+ colleges stay vacant after the scheduled allocation rounds — students who got better offers elsewhere, those who didn't complete document verification in time, or seats in less-popular combinations that simply didn't fill up. That's exactly what DU Spot Admission 2026 exists for.

This isn't a guaranteed seat, and it isn't a repeat of the regular process. Spot admission runs differently, moves faster, and rewards candidates who are prepared and flexible about college and programme choice. Here's exactly how it works.

## What Is DU Spot Admission?

Once the University of Delhi completes its regular CSAS UG allocation-cum-admission rounds (typically two to three rounds), any seats that remain unfilled are thrown open through one or more Spot Admission Rounds. DU publishes a list of vacant seats — college-wise and programme-wise — on the CSAS portal, and eligible candidates can apply directly for those seats.

Unlike the regular rounds, where an algorithm allots seats based on your preferences and merit, spot admission works more like first-come, first-served within eligibility: you pick from what's actually vacant, not from a ranked preference list.

## Who Can Apply for Spot Admission 2026

- You registered for DU CSAS UG 2026 using your CUET UG 2026 application number.
- You are not currently admitted to any DU programme (or you've withdrawn from your allotted seat).
- You meet the minimum eligibility and CUET-based cutoff for the specific programme you're applying to in the spot round.

If you already have a seat allotted and accepted, you generally cannot apply for spot admission unless you first withdraw — and withdrawing has consequences, so weigh that decision carefully before acting.

## Step-by-Step: How to Apply

### 1. Check the vacant seat list

DU publishes the updated vacancy matrix — college, programme, category-wise seats — on the official CSAS portal (admission.uod.ac.in) and on du.ac.in. Check this before anything else; it tells you what's realistically available.

### 2. Log in to the CSAS portal

Use your existing CUET UG 2026 application number and CSAS login credentials. There's no fresh registration needed if you already registered for CSAS UG 2026.

### 3. Opt for the Spot Admission window

Select the 'Spot Admission' option when it opens on the portal, and choose from the colleges/programmes showing vacant seats that match your eligibility.

### 4. Accept the seat and complete verification

If offered a seat, accept it within the given window, upload the required documents for verification, and pay the admission fee before the deadline. Missing this deadline cancels the offer instantly — there's no reminder round.

### 5. No upgrade, no withdrawal

This is the biggest difference from regular rounds: once you accept a spot-round seat, you cannot upgrade to a "better" vacancy in a later spot round, and withdrawal options are either unavailable or heavily restricted. Choose only if you're genuinely willing to join.

| Class XII marksheet | Academic eligibility and tie-breaking |
| CUET UG 2026 scorecard | Basis for programme eligibility |

| Passport-size photo & signature | Portal upload requirement |

## Fees to Keep in Mind

The CSAS UG registration fee (₹250 for UR/OBC-NCL/EWS, ₹100 for SC/ST/PwBD) is a one-time, non-refundable payment made during initial registration — you won't pay it again for spot admission if you've already registered. What you will pay is the college admission fee once your spot-round seat is confirmed, along with any programme-specific fee (for example, BFA, B.Sc. Physical Education, or B.A. Hons Music carry an extra ₹400; ECA/Sports quota applications add ₹100 each).

## Tips to Actually Get a Seat

- Be flexible on college. A strong programme at a lesser-known DU college is still a DU degree with full university recognition.
- Be flexible on combination. If your first-choice subject combination didn't come through, look at closely related programmes with vacant seats.
- Keep documents ready in advance. Spot rounds move in hours, not days — scrambling for a scanned certificate can cost you the seat.
- Track the portal daily. DU sometimes runs more than one spot round if seats remain after the first.
- Don't ignore off-campus and evening colleges. Vacancies are often concentrated there, and the academic quality is university-standard.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is DU Spot Admission the same as a regular CSAS round?**
No. Spot admission only opens after all regular rounds are over, applies only to seats left vacant, and has no upgrade or withdrawal facility once you accept.

**Can I apply for spot admission if I never registered for CSAS UG 2026?**
No. You must have completed CSAS UG 2026 registration with your CUET UG 2026 application number before the spot round opens.

**How many spot rounds does DU usually conduct?**
This varies year to year depending on how many seats remain vacant — DU has conducted one to two spot rounds in recent admission cycles, announced separately on the CSAS portal.

**Will I get my preferred college in spot admission?**
Not necessarily. You can only choose among seats currently shown as vacant, which may not include your first-choice college or programme.

**What happens if I don't pay the fee or verify documents in time?**
Your spot-round offer is cancelled automatically, and the seat goes back into the vacancy pool for any subsequent round.

## Final Word

Missing all the regular CSAS rounds feels like the end of the road, but DU Spot Admission 2026 is a real, functioning path back in — provided you're quick, flexible, and prepared with your documents. The seats available won't always match your first choice, but a DU degree from any of its constituent colleges carries the same university recognition and much of the same campus life, societies, and placement access. Treat the spot round as a genuine opportunity, not a consolation prize, and move the moment the vacancy list goes live.
