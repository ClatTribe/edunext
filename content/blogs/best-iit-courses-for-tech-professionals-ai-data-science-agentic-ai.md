AI roles increasingly overlap. A data professional may need stronger deep learning and deployment skills, while a software engineer may need RAG, tool calling, memory, and agent orchestration. For experienced professionals, choosing an AI program now depends on which technical layer needs the most attention.

Some IIT programs provide a broad path through statistics, machine learning, and Generative AI. Others are more specialized, concentrating on autonomous agents, MCP, LangGraph, multi-agent coordination, and production monitoring.

The five programs below cover both directions, making it easier to compare a deeper AI and data science pathway with focused Agentic AI training.

## 1. Certificate in Agentic AI - IIT Bombay

The **IIT [Agentic AI Course](https://www.mygreatlearning.com/iit-bombay-certificate-in-agentic-ai)** is designed for professionals moving from LLM applications into autonomous systems. Python concurrency, APIs, transformers, and structured prompting establish the base before learners work with tool calling, memory, RAG, GraphRAG, MCP, LangGraph, CrewAI, reasoning, and multi-agent coordination.

**Delivery & Duration:** Five months, fully online, with weekly live IIT Bombay faculty sessions, guided labs, projects, and an optional one-day campus immersion.

**Credentials:** Certificate of Completion from IIT Bombay.

**Program Highlights:** AsyncIO, router patterns, vector databases, MCP, LangGraph, CrewAI, CoT, ReAct, Plan-and-Solve, reflection, DSPy, multi-agent handoffs, LangSmith, guardrails, FastAPI, Streamlit, and Docker.

**Outcomes:** Learners develop agents that plan, reason, use tools, share workflow knowledge, collaborate with other agents, and operate with monitoring and human oversight.

### Why should you choose this course?

- **It covers the complete agent lifecycle.** Memory and orchestration progress into evaluation, prompt security, observability, and deployment.
- **The projects increase in autonomy.** Learners eventually work with multi-agent collaboration and an agent-based software engineering workflow.

## 2. Applied Machine Learning and Agentic AI: Fundamentals to Next Generation Artificial Intelligence - IIT Kanpur

IIT Kanpur takes a compressed route from conventional AI foundations into newer agent systems. Python and statistical concepts lead into machine learning, neural networks, transformers, LLM infrastructure, RAG, LangChain, and an Agentic AI build track.

**Delivery & Duration:** Seven weeks with 75+ learning hours, faculty discussions, prerecorded material, doubt-solving sessions, and 15+ hands-on projects.

**Credentials:** Certificate from IIT Kanpur.

**Program Highlights:** Regression, CART, clustering, CNNs, RNNs, autoencoders, transformers, Mixture of Experts, Hugging Face, vLLM, Ollama, prompt engineering, RAG, and AI agent tooling.

**Outcomes:** Participants move from predictive modeling into LLM serving and retrieval-enabled autonomous applications.

### Why should you choose this course?

- **It connects ML with modern AI infrastructure** without requiring a year-long commitment.
- **Model development does not stand alone.** LLM serving, retrieval, and agents extend the learning into application engineering.

## 3. e-Postgraduate Diploma in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science - IIT Bombay

This **[AI and data science course](https://www.mygreatlearning.com/iit-bombay-pg-diploma-ai-data-science)** takes a broader academic route. Its six-course, 36-credit curriculum covers programming, statistical foundations, machine learning, deep learning, Generative AI, AI-ML deployment, and an elective in NLP, IoT, or computer vision.

**Delivery & Duration:** Designed for completion in 18 months, with about 12 to 14 hours of weekly study and in-person end-term examinations at IIT Bombay.

**Credentials:** e-Postgraduate Diploma in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science from IIT Bombay.

**Program Highlights:** Python, regression, classification, clustering, PCA, neural networks, TensorFlow/Keras or PyTorch, transformers, LLM fine-tuning, transfer learning, Docker, Kubernetes, cloud deployment, and a group capstone.

**Outcomes:** Learners build and deploy end-to-end AI/ML solutions while developing stronger skills across data analysis, machine learning, GenAI, and production infrastructure.

### Why should you choose this course?

- **It provides substantially more depth in data science and ML** than a short agent-focused certificate.
- **AI-ML in Practice adds the production layer** through cloud deployment, Docker, Kubernetes, and a capstone.

## 4. Applied AI & Agentic Systems - E&ICT Academy, IIT Roorkee

IIT Roorkee begins with software engineering and ML before moving into LLM applications. Python, FastAPI, SQL, data analysis, and machine learning are followed by RAG and a 14-week Agentic Systems and Production stage.

**Delivery & Duration:** Ten months with live online classes, recordings, projects, Build Week, and optional campus immersion.

**Credentials:** Certificate in Applied AI & Agentic Systems from E&ICT Academy, IIT Roorkee.

**Program Highlights:** FastAPI, PyTorch, embeddings, vector search, LangChain, LangGraph, MCP, Ragas, promptfoo, LangSmith, Docker, agent evaluation, and production deployment.

**Outcomes:** Learners build RAG pipelines, LangGraph agents, evaluation workflows, and a deployed capstone with human approval for higher-risk actions.

### Why should you choose this course?

- **It builds software engineering foundations before agent orchestration.**
- **Evaluation and safety have dedicated curriculum time**, including tracing, hallucinations, prompt injection, and deployment testing.

## 5. Professional Certificate Program in Agentic AI and Applications - IITM Pravartak

IITM Pravartak focuses more directly on goal-driven AI systems. LLMs and embeddings lead into agent frameworks, planning, memory, MCP, advanced RAG, adaptation, monitoring, debugging, and governance.

**Delivery & Duration:** Twenty weeks online with live expert sessions, IIT Madras guest faculty masterclasses, 20+ projects and cases, and a capstone.

**Credentials:** Professional certificate from IITM Pravartak Technologies Foundation.

**Program Highlights:** LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen, FAISS, Flowise, memory and knowledge retrieval, MCP, adaptive instructions, advanced RAG, agent evaluation, monitoring, and low-code tools.

**Outcomes:** Learners design agents that retain knowledge, use tools, coordinate across multi-agent workflows, adapt through feedback, and move toward deployable applications.

### Why should you choose this course?

- **Memory and MCP receive dedicated treatment**, rather than appearing only as supporting RAG concepts.
- **The capstone brings planning, tools, retrieval, and agent behavior together** in a practical build.

## Conclusion

The right IIT program depends on whether the immediate need is deeper AI foundations or more specialized autonomous-system skills. Data science and ML depth remains important, but experienced professionals may instead need RAG, memory, MCP, evaluation, and orchestration.

When comparing AI courses, consider where each curriculum begins and where it finishes. A broader AI and data science program can strengthen modeling and deployment, while a focused Agentic AI certificate may make more sense when the next goal is designing and operating autonomous systems.
