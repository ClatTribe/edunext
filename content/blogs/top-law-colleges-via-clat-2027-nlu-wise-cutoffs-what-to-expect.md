# Top Law Colleges via CLAT 2027: NLU-Wise Cutoffs and What to Expect

**Key Takeaways**

- **CLAT 2027 will be held on December 6, 2026** (2 PM–4 PM, offline mode), with registration open from **August 3 to October 31, 2026** on consortiumofnlus.ac.in.
- **26 NLUs** will participate in CLAT 2027 — up from 24 in CLAT 2026 — with NLU Tripura (Agartala) and NLU Meghalaya (Shillong) newly inducted.
- CLAT 2027 cutoffs aren't out yet; the most reliable benchmark is the **CLAT 2026 Round 1 general-category closing ranks**, which this guide breaks down NLU-wise.
- NLSIU Bengaluru remains the most competitive seat in the country, closing at **AIR 101** in Round 1 of CLAT 2026.
- Cutoffs shift across counselling rounds and years, so treat these numbers as a planning reference, not a guarantee.

<h2 id="clat-2027-overview">CLAT 2027: Dates and What's Changed</h2>

CLAT 2027 — the entrance test for undergraduate and postgraduate law admissions at India's National Law Universities — is scheduled for **December 6, 2026**, conducted offline by the Consortium of NLUs. Registration is open from **August 3 to October 31, 2026** at consortiumofnlus.ac.in, so if you're targeting this cycle, the clock on your application window has already started.

The biggest structural change for 2027 is scale: **26 NLUs** are now part of the consortium, with NLU Tripura (Agartala) and NLU Meghalaya (Shillong) joining the fold. More participating NLUs generally means a modest widening of the overall seat pool, though it doesn't meaningfully ease competition at the very top — NLSIU, NALSAR and NLU Delhi (via AILET) remain disproportionately oversubscribed relative to seats available.

> **EduNext tip:** More NLUs in the consortium is good news if you're aiming for a solid law degree outside the top 5 — cutoffs at newer and mid-tier NLUs tend to be far more forgiving than at the marquee names.

<h2 id="why-past-cutoffs-matter">Why CLAT 2026 Cutoffs Are Your Best Reference Point</h2>

CLAT 2027 cutoffs won't exist until results are declared in December 2026, so the only meaningful way to set a target rank today is to study the most recent actual data — **CLAT 2026's Round 1 general-category closing ranks**. Cutoffs move a little each year based on the number of applicants, paper difficulty, and seat matrix changes, but historically the movement between consecutive years has been within a reasonably narrow band for established NLUs, making last year's numbers a workable planning anchor.

<h2 id="factors-affecting-2027-cutoffs">What Could Move CLAT 2027 Cutoffs</h2>

A few factors are worth watching as CLAT 2027 approaches, since they typically shift cutoffs year to year:

- **Total applicant volume.** A jump or drop in registrations directly affects how competitive each rank band becomes.
- **Paper difficulty.** A harder CLAT 2027 paper generally compresses the score distribution, which can shift rank-to-percentile mapping even if your relative performance is similar to past years.
- **Seat matrix changes.** With NLU Tripura and NLU Meghalaya added, the total UG seat count rises — a factor that tends to modestly ease cutoffs across the board, though top-tier NLUs are largely insulated from this effect.
- **Counselling round-wise movement.** Closing ranks in Round 1 are always tighter than final-round closing ranks, since higher-rank candidates who hold multiple offers gradually vacate seats as rounds progress.

> **Honest trade-off:** Chasing only the top 5 NLUs based on last year's cutoff can backfire if your CLAT 2027 rank lands just outside that band. Building a realistic preference list across 12–15 NLUs, not just the household names, protects you from an all-or-nothing outcome.

<h2 id="preparation-tips">Setting a Realistic Target for CLAT 2027</h2>

1. **Anchor your target rank to this year's Round 1 data**, not the final-round cutoff — Round 1 numbers are the more conservative, safer benchmark to prepare against.
2. **Build a preference list across tiers**, not just the top 5, since mid-tier NLUs like HNLU, NLIU, and RGNUL offer strong placement outcomes at meaningfully more accessible ranks.
3. **Track official cutoff releases only** at consortiumofnlus.ac.in once CLAT 2027 results are out — third-party predictions before the official list are estimates, not guarantees.
4. **Register within the window** (August 3–October 31, 2026) and confirm your category and reservation documentation early, since counselling moves fast once results are declared.
